## DM Overview

Three acts. The first finishes Session 18's remaining content — Zone 4 and the reward scene at Elira's cellar. The second is the planning phase: Elira guides the party toward the Arcanum infiltration with the firsthand knowledge of someone who works inside the building every day and the urgency of someone who knows the window is closing. The third is the heist itself, or the beginning of it if planning runs long.

The session's emotional centre is the cellar scene and what follows it. Elira is correct about everything. She looks like she is not holding together well. The party has to decide how much to trust her — and then act.

Samson's Arcanum review notice arrives during this session. It is both a complication and an asset.

## Elira's Cellar — The Reward Scene

_Budget 25–35 minutes. This replaces the brief debrief exit scene from Session 18's original outline._

She leads them through her townhouse — narrow, unremarkable from the street — and straight downstairs without stopping to explain. She lights two lamps from a tinderbox. There are no enchanted sconces down here. She does not like sustained magical light near her working materials.

### The Room

> _The cellar is the working room. Every wall is pinned — not a tidy board, not a display, but the walls themselves, directly: maps layered on older maps until the original geography is barely visible beneath four revisions; string running in at least three overlapping systems that only partially agree with each other; notes written in margins and then crossed out and then rewritten over the crossing-out. Dates are everywhere. Some are circled. Some have question marks. Some have been revised so many times they are just dark patches of ink._
>
> _The floor is mostly clear, which is the only concession to occupancy. A central table is buried under stacked documents. A pile of books near the far wall has been repurposed as a writing surface — the top volume is open face-down, spine cracked, with a single note pinned to its cover that reads only: **"CHECK THIS."** The note is not recent._
>
> _She sits at the table without gesture. She opens the construction ledger and sets the surveyor's rod beside it. She does not wait to see if the party has found chairs._

Let the room land. Give the players a moment to look around. If anyone examines something specific, they can — but the organizational system is not legible to an outsider. A DC 15 Investigation reveals the broad shape of it: the whole thing is arranged radially from a central point on the largest map. The central point is the Cradle Tower.

### How She Presents

She is warm, precise, and too fast. She references things she has not explained yet and then has to circle back. She gets three thoughts ahead of her own sentences. She pulls documents from specific spots in the stacks with absolute confidence — she knows exactly where everything is, even though no one else can see the system.

She has not been sleeping well. She does not slow down.

Moments that read as unhinged but are reasonable:
- _"I stopped using the third-floor archive reading room three weeks ago. Vos's deputy uses the same corridor at noon and I cannot be certain what he notices or reports."_ This is a sensible precaution for someone in her position. It sounds like paranoia.
- She refers to the Undertow Programme as "what it actually was" before she has explained what the Undertow Programme is, then catches herself and backs up without embarrassment.
- If the party surrendered the Crownwatch sigil from the lockbox, she takes it from them without asking and places it directly at the centre of the map — on top of the Cradle Tower marker — as if it belongs there. She does not explain why. She moves on immediately.

The goal is: she is right about everything. The party does not have to know that yet. Let them carry a quiet _"...is she alright?"_ out of this scene. That doubt pays off.

### What She Shares

Present this as a conversation, not a monologue. Let players ask and let her answer readily. All of the following should land before the scene closes — if players ask nothing, she volunteers it anyway, but player questions make it feel like they're keeping up.

**She states plainly:**

- Verrath. Name, role, division. "High Arcanist. Magister Primus of the Deep Research Index. His division built what you were standing in." She says this as settled fact, not a theory. She has had evidence for this for some time. She was waiting until she could hold it.
- The relay network leads to the Cradle Tower. "That is not a theory anymore. You confirmed it in Zone 4."
- The construction notation matches research the Arcanum officially suspended eleven years ago. The programme has a name: the Undertow Programme. "It was not abandoned. It was buried. There is a difference."
- She is an archivist. She works inside the Arcanum. She is in the Academic Library most days. She does not have access to the Deep Research Index — Verrath's division — without submitting a formal authorization request. "If I walk up to that desk and ask to see his files, my name is on a request form in a division I have been very carefully not alerting. I cannot do it myself."

**Her theory, flagged as theory:**

- "I do not think he invented this. The notation in Zone 3 predates anything he built. I think he found something older and built on top of it — which is why he believes his version will succeed where the original programme failed. He thinks he understands something they did not."
- She does not know what the Undertow Programme's failure actually looked like. She suspects it was worse than the file language suggests. "When institutional records call something a 'structural event,' that is a phrase chosen by someone who needed to control what got out."

**What she genuinely does not know:**

- What is specifically in the Deep Research Index files
- Whether Verrath knows the party has been in the sewers
- Who the partially-redacted name on the original suspension order is _(do not raise this — it is for the heist payoff)_

### Payment

Gold as agreed. She pays without ceremony. She is already on to the next problem.

## Samson's Review Notice

Delivered this session — slipped under the door at wherever the party is staying, or waiting for Samson when they surface from the sewers, handed off by a junior Arcanum official who tracked him down professionally. Institutional, brief:

> _"The court of Magistrate-Auditor Cael Reno has submitted a record of conduct involving a registered Arcanum student during a civil restraint proceeding. You are requested to present yourself for a conduct review at your earliest available opportunity. The reviewing officer for this matter is Dean Kelveth Ravos, second floor, Room 7. Attendance is not optional."_

If Elira is shown this — or told about it — she reads it twice. Then:

> _"Ravos. Enchantment and Illusion."_ A pause. _"He does not usually handle student conduct reviews."_

She does not say what that means. She lets the inference sit. If pressed: "He is not the dean you would send for a routine matter. Someone chose him specifically." She does not know who made that choice or why. That is the point.

## The Planning Phase

_Budget 30–40 minutes. This is the session's central act._

Elira has current, firsthand knowledge of the Arcanum — she is inside the building most days. She will share what she knows, answer questions directly, and point out gaps the party hasn't thought of. She will not make their plan for them. She will probe gaps without filling them: _"How are you getting to the sub-level?" — "And the Division Key — do you know where it is held?" — "Does Samson know what Ravos is actually looking for?"_

### What She Volunteers Without Being Asked

- **Layout:** Main building, six floors, elevator in the central shaft. Academic Library — her section — is ground floor east. Lecture halls ground floor west. Floors 1–2 are academic: study halls, junior offices, review rooms. Floor 3 is faculty: senior offices, council chambers, and the covered bridge connecting to the Cradle Tower. Sub-level 1 is specimen storage. Sub-level 2 is the Deep Research Index.
- **The elevator:** Arcane-powered. Requires authorization tokens — small brass discs keyed to floors. Ground floor tokens are given freely at reception. Student ID covers floors 1–2. Faculty sigil for floor 3. Sub-levels require a Division Key — a physical token held by the division head of each section.
- **The Division Key for Section IV** (Verrath's division) is likely in the Section IV division head's office on floor 3. She does not know exactly where in that office.
- **Her position:** She works there. She cannot be the one to go in. "If something goes wrong and my name is connected to this, I lose everything I have built and they know how much I know. You go. I give you what I can."
- **Samson's review:** His appointment is on floor 2, Room 7. That is inside the building. That is useful.
- **Patrol timing:** Guards rotate every 30 minutes on the ground floor. Sub-level patrols pass every 45 minutes during the day, every hour at night.

### Common Questions and Answers

**"Can you just go in yourself?"**
She can enter the Arcanum freely — she works there. She cannot access the Deep Research Index without a formal request that would flag her name to Verrath's division. "I have been inside that building hundreds of times. I cannot take this particular step."

**"Can Samson get us into the archive?"**
His student credentials cover floors 1–2. The archive is sub-level 2. He would need a Division Key or someone who has one.

**"Who is Ithon Relos?"**
Dean of Divination and Historical Magic, floor 3. She knew him years ago — before she started this investigation, before she became careful. He was privately uncomfortable with certain directions the Arcanum was taking. She has not spoken to him since she began this work. "He is the kind of person who might help if you approach him correctly. He is also the kind of person who will close the door in your face if you approach him wrong, and you will not get a second chance." Correct approach: show him specific evidence. The surveyor's rod, the ledger notation. Something real. Not vague suspicions. Wrong approach: anything abstract, anything that asks him to take a position before he has seen proof.

**"What can Relos actually do for us?"**
If he agrees to help: he can schedule a faculty meeting at a specific time, which would pull Ravos and archive staff away from their posts — a window of 20–30 minutes in which floor 3 is quieter and the archive staff are occupied. He might also know exactly where the Division Key is. He will not accompany them. He will not get his hands dirty. But he can open a door.

**"Can we just go in through the front?"**
Magister Alren Vos is at reception. Professional, thorough. Every visitor without a scheduled appointment is logged by name and purpose. "If something is disturbed in the archive later, those names are the first thing an investigator checks."

**"What about the service entrance?"**
West side of the building, maintenance access. No sign-in, no log. She knows about it; maintenance and construction workers use it. The construct patrols on that route are heavier — two pass through every 20 minutes — but constructs check credentials and behavior, not faces. "If you look like you belong and you keep moving, they will not stop you. If you hesitate, they will."

**"What if we go at night?"**
Sub-level patrol frequency drops to every hour. But the day shift archive staff leave and one archivist takes the night shift alone. She knows who: an older man named Torvin Aster. She worked alongside him for three years. "He is not loyal to Verrath specifically. He is loyal to procedure. He is present and attentive. He is a complication, not an enemy — unless you make him one."

**"What about the Crownwatch sigil?"**
She does not recommend this. "That sigil belongs to a specific person. Someone from Crownwatch was in those tunnels. If you use their sigil as a bluff and the wrong person recognizes it, you have a different problem than the one you started with. I would not unless you have no other option."

**"What does Ravos actually want from Samson?"**
"He will start with the court incident. That is the pretext. Then he will ask about Samson's associations outside the Arcanum, whether he has noticed anything unusual in the city, why he was in Smokestack Row. He is looking for what Samson knows — not what he did." She tells Samson directly: go in knowing what questions are coming. Prepare answers that are technically true and reveal nothing. "If you are surprised by what he asks, you will show it, and Ravos will notice."

**"What is actually in the files?"**
"The original Undertow Programme research. An incident report from the test they ran eleven years ago — the one that ended the programme. I believe there is documentation of what Phase II actually consists of, because Verrath files everything. He does not operate without a record." She does not know the format. She does not know what the incident report calls what happened. "That is what I need you to find."

**"What if we're caught?"**
She walks through it honestly. Found somewhere you should not be, with a plausible cover story: escorted back to an appropriate area, no lasting consequence. Caught somewhere you cannot explain: logged, escorted out of the building, names on an incident report. Caught in the archive with documents: the Arcanum Guard treats that as theft. "I cannot help you if it reaches that point. You would be on your own in the moment."

**"How long do we have once we're in the archive?"**
"A targeted search, knowing the division and what you are looking for, should take fifteen to twenty minutes if nothing goes wrong. The patrol window on the sub-level is forty-five minutes during the day. You need to be in and out within one window." She pauses. "You need either the patrol schedule confirmed or a distraction. Ideally both."

### What Good Planning Looks Like

The party does not need to think of all of this. Every decision they make or skip creates a variable in the heist. See [[The Arcanum Heist]] for how each plays out.

**Decisions that make it easier:**
- Contact Relos before going in, with real evidence
- Enter via service entrance or Relos's invitation (no visitor log)
- Locate or obtain the Division Key before the heist
- Scout the building or confirm current patrol timing with Elira
- Brief Samson on Ravos's likely questions

**Decisions that create problems:**
- Skipping Relos entirely
- Entering via the front without a plan for the visitor log
- Going in without a Division Key (requires two risky arcane bypasses in sequence)
- Samson walking in unprepared
