# The Arcanum Heist

## Alert Level

Track this as a single number throughout the heist. Start at 0. This determines what consequences apply when things go wrong.

| Level | State | What It Means |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Clean | No incidents. The party moves through accessible areas without issue. |
| 1 | Noticed | One incident flagged. Guards are more attentive; an extra patrol passes within 20 minutes. |
| 2 | Sought | Two incidents, or one serious one. The party is known to be somewhere they should not be. A guard is actively looking. Patrols increase to every 20 minutes. |
| 3 | Caught | Direct confrontation. Escalates to warning, ejection, or combat depending on party response. |

**Raises Alert Level by 1:**
- Failed Stealth check that a guard notices
- Found in a restricted area without a plausible cover story
- Triggering an arcane ward
- Caught a second time after already being at Level 1

**Immediately goes to Level 3:**
- Found in the archive with documents in hand

## Building Layout

The Royal Arcanum occupies a large complex in Scholar's Descent. The main building — the Tower of Magisters — is six storeys of pale stone and blue-tinted glass. The Cradle Tower is older, narrower, attached to the main building by a covered bridge on the third floor. The elevator shaft runs through the centre of the Tower of Magisters.

### Ground Floor — Public Zone
- **Main entrance:** two Arcanum Guard posts flanking the doors; Magister Alren Vos or a deputy at the reception desk behind a wide stone counter
- **Academic Library (east wing):** Elira's section; public-accessible with sign-in at a separate desk; tall arched bookshelves, blue-glass sconces, hushed
- **Lecture halls (west wing):** publicly accessible, usually occupied during the day
- **Elevator (central shaft):** the panel and call-button are visible in the central hall; the elevator will not move without an authorization token inserted into the wall slot
- **Service entrance (west exterior):** a maintenance door off a narrow side alley; no sign-in desk; construct patrols every 20 minutes

### Floors 1–2 — Academic Zone
- Student study halls and seminar rooms
- Junior faculty offices (closed doors, knock-before-entering norm)
- **Restricted Reading Room (floor 2, east corridor):** locked with a physical key held by the floor deputy at the corridor desk; requires academic clearance
- **Review Room 7 (floor 2):** where Samson's appointment with Ravos takes place; a plain office with a desk and two chairs; Ravos arrives first

### Floor 3 — Faculty Zone
- Senior faculty offices along a single corridor; doors are closed; most faculty are present during the day
- **Council chambers:** locked outside scheduled meeting hours
- **Section IV Office — Room 14 (far end of the corridor):** the division head's office for the Deep Research Index; this is where the Division Key is kept; the room is locked (DC 13 thieves' tools or DC 14 Sleight of Hand to enter quietly); the Division Key is in the centre desk drawer (unlocked)
- **The covered bridge to the Cradle Tower:** at the corridor's north end; a checkpoint here with two senior guards; no passage without Verrath's personal authorization; the guards do not negotiate

### Sub-level 1 — Specimen Vaults
Artifact storage, magical creature containment. Not part of this plan.

### Sub-level 2 — Deep Research Index
- A stone corridor, dimly lit by torchlight sconces — the enchanted ones flicker unreliably down here and have been replaced with mundane flame
- **Guard checkpoint at the corridor entrance:** a single guard seated at a small desk with a logbook; checks credentials and logs entry by name
- **Archive door:** heavy oak and iron, at the corridor's end; a brass Division Key lock and a secondary arcane glyph above the frame
  - Division Key bypasses both automatically; the lock clicks and the glyph dims
  - Without the key: the elevator cannot be directed here without a DC 18 Arcana check, and the glyph requires a separate DC 16 Arcana check to suppress — both in sequence, under time pressure, in a narrow corridor
- **Inside:** long stone shelves organized by division (Roman numerals carved into shelf ends) and date range. Section IV begins at the third row from the left. The shelves are full but orderly. The filing system is logical once understood.

### The Cradle Tower
Verrath's sanctum. Connected to the main building by the third-floor bridge. Separately guarded. Not reachable this session. Whatever is in the sub-basement of the Cradle Tower — the relay anchor terminus — is not part of this plan. Do not let the party get onto that bridge.

## The Elevator

Arcane-powered, slow, and audible. It emits a low tonal hum when moving that can be heard from 30 feet. The shaft has no intermediate openings — only the elevator car, which stops only at requested floors.

Authorization tokens are small brass discs stamped with a floor glyph. Insert into the wall panel slot; the elevator travels to the highest authorized floor the inserted token permits.

| Token | Coverage | Where to Get It |
|---|---|---|
| Ground floor token | Ground only (free movement) | Available from reception without question |
| Academic token | Floors 1–2 | Student ID badge functions as this |
| Faculty token | Floor 3 | Faculty only; the Section IV office has one on the desk beside the key |
| Division Key | Sub-level 2 | Section IV Office, Room 14 |

**Without a valid token for the destination:** DC 18 Arcana to operate the elevator manually. The hum becomes louder and arrhythmic during manual operation. Any guard within 60 feet who is paying attention may notice (DC 14 Perception).

## Planning Variables — How Prep Affects the Heist

Each decision the party made during planning (or skipped) maps to a specific moment in the heist.

| Decision | Made Well | Skipped or Failed |
|---|---|---|
| Contact Relos with evidence | Faculty meeting distraction: 25-min window, floor 3 corridor quiet, Ravos occupied | No distraction; full staff present; Ravos may return to his office mid-heist |
| Entry route | No visitor log entry; party not on record | Names logged at reception; names surface in any investigation |
| Division Key | Sub-level access is clean and silent | Two DC checks in sequence (18 and 16 Arcana) in a narrow corridor |
| Patrol scouting | Know the real rotation; time movements correctly | Patrol arrives unexpectedly at a bad moment |
| Brief Samson on Ravos | Review goes cleanly; Samson free to join party after | Ravos presses hard; Samson may give something away or be watched afterward |

## Entry

### Route A — Main Entrance
Through the front doors. Vos or his deputy is at reception. Samson is logged as attending a review — no issue for him. Others need one of:
- A written faculty invitation from Relos (bypasses Vos's questioning entirely)
- A visitor permit (DC 14 Persuasion; Vos asks purpose, organization, who they're meeting; success = logged by name, failure = turned away with a note made)

> **Vos, if addressed:** Precise, unhurried, professionally pleasant. He has seen every type of person walk through those doors. He is not hostile. He is thorough. If the cover story has a gap, he finds it — not accusatorially, but with a follow-up question that requires a specific answer.

**Outcome:** Names on the visitor log create a paper trail. If anything is disturbed later, those names are reviewed first.

### Route B — Service Entrance
West alley, a plain maintenance door. No log, no reception. Constructs patrol every 20 minutes in pairs. They respond to behavior and credentials, not faces.

If the party looks purposeful and equipped appropriately (maintenance gear, tools, moving with intent): DC 12 Deception or simply walking through without hesitation works.

If they hesitate, look around, or engage the constructs conversationally: the constructs halt them for a credentials check. DC 16 Persuasion to redirect; failure = Alert +1 and the constructs remember their appearance.

> **The constructs:** Roughly humanoid, brass-plated, eye lenses that glow faintly amber. They do not speak. They stop. They wait. Their silence is the most unsettling thing about them. They will resume their patrol if credentials check out.

**Outcome:** No log entry. Heavier construct presence. The party enters the west service corridor, which connects to the ground floor near the elevator shaft's side panel.

### Route C — Faculty Invitation (Relos)
If the party approached Relos beforehand and he agreed:

- **Good approach (showed him real evidence — the surveyor's rod, the ledger):** He provides a written invitation letter and schedules a compulsory faculty meeting during the heist window, pulling Ravos and the sub-level archive staff. He tells them where the Division Key is (Room 14, centre desk drawer) and confirms current patrol timing. He meets them briefly at the Academic Library side entrance and says nothing more. His name is not on anything that happens next.

- **Acceptable approach (professional, no hard evidence):** He provides the invitation letter only. No distraction, no further information.

- **Poor approach or skipped:** No Relos involvement. The door was closed. He did not alert anyone — he is not hostile, just absent.

## Moving Through the Building

### Ground Floor to Elevator
The elevator panel is visible from Vos's reception desk. If party members approach the elevator without apparent purpose, Vos will inquire pleasantly from across the hall. Having Samson as cover works — he has a legitimate appointment and can accompany others to the elevator naturally.

The elevator car, once called, takes approximately 45 seconds to arrive. It is not discreet.

### Floors 1–2 — Academic Zone
Navigable with student credentials or a visitor log entry. One guard per floor on a 30-minute rotation. Samson moves here freely. The floor deputy at the floor 2 corridor desk is present 9am–5pm; she monitors traffic to the Restricted Reading Room and review offices but does not interrogate visitors with proper log entries.

> _Floor 2 feels institutional. Identical wooden doors, numbered brass plates. The carpet is worn in the centre of the corridor from years of the same foot traffic. It smells faintly of old parchment and something chemical — preservation solution from the reading room._

### Floor 3 — Faculty Zone
Requires faculty credentials for the elevator (the Division Key or a separate faculty token), or use of the east stairwell (not locked, but unusual for non-faculty; DC 13 Deception if a faculty member is encountered).

If Relos called a meeting: the corridor is notably quieter. A few office doors are shut and dark. The meeting window is approximately 25–30 minutes from start.

If no meeting: two or three faculty members may be in their offices; doors closed; knock-before-entering norm means the party has a brief moment before anyone sees them at Room 14.

**Section IV Office — Room 14:**
Locked. DC 13 thieves' tools to open; or DC 14 Sleight of Hand if attempting to slip the latch with something thin. Inside: a plain faculty office, desk covered in neatly organized folders, a bookshelf of bound research volumes. The Division Key is in the centre desk drawer — unlocked, sitting beside a pen case. A faculty elevator token is on the desk surface. The party can take both.

> _The office has the quality of a room used mainly for storage. Whoever works here does not linger. There is nothing personal on the desk. The only thing that distinguishes it from any other office on this floor is a framed organizational chart on the wall — the Deep Research Index, its divisions listed by section number. Section IV is circled in pencil. Verrath's name appears at the top._

### Sub-level Corridor
The elevator deposits them into a stone corridor. Dimly lit. Cooler than the floors above. The torchlight sconces cast uneven shadows; the enchanted replacements that were installed here two years ago have been quietly failing and no one has fixed them.

**The checkpoint guard** sits at a small desk ten feet from the elevator doors. He has a logbook open and a quill ready. He is not hostile — this is a routine position and he expects routine visitors. He will ask for credentials and enter the visit in the log.

Options:
- **Division Key:** He examines it, nods, logs a generic "division access" entry, and waves them through. He does not ask for names if they do not volunteer them. (Elira's intel was right — the Key is the cleanest route.)
- **No Key, persuasion attempt:** DC 16 Persuasion, with a +2 if Samson is present and uses his student status. Success = he logs them by name and lets them through. Failure = he tells them he cannot authorize access without proper credentials and reaches for a speaking tube to call the floor supervisor. Alert +1 if they do not immediately back off.
- **Stealth past him:** The desk is positioned to watch the elevator doors. There is no route that bypasses him without magic or a distraction. DC 18 Stealth if the party attempts to slip past — he is not distracted by default.

**Sub-level patrol:** Every 45 minutes during the day (every hour at night). Single guard, walking the corridor from the checkpoint to the archive door and back. Roll or track timing from when the party enters the sub-level.

## The Archive

The archive door opens easily once the Division Key is applied — the lock disengages, the glyph fades, and the door swings inward on heavy hinges.

Inside, the air is drier and noticeably cooler. Stone shelves run floor to ceiling, organized by section number. The Roman numerals are carved directly into the shelf ends. Section IV begins at the third row.

**Finding the Undertow Programme files:** DC 13 Investigation, or automatic if Relos told them the filing system. The section is organized by year and classification. "Suspended Research" is a subsection marker about halfway along Section IV's shelves.

**Time pressure:** The party has the duration of one patrol window from when they entered the sub-level. A targeted search takes approximately 15–20 minutes if they know what to look for. If they are searching without direction: add 10 minutes per vague search attempt.

> _The shelves here smell different from the floors above — not parchment but something older. Stone and time. The torchlight inside the archive is steadier than the corridor sconces, at least. Whatever enchantment was applied to these particular sconces was done carefully. This room was designed to last._

### The Three Folders

**Folder 1 — Undertow Programme: Theoretical Foundation (Years 1–2)**

Dense arcane theory. Readable with DC 14 Arcana or History. Key content: the programme was attempting to compress ambient ley energy from beneath the city into a directed release — not a weapon initially, but a power source for large-scale arcane infrastructure projects. The theoretical framework is sound. The first two years of records are clean, professional, and thorough.

A margin note in one of the Year 2 documents reads, in small careful handwriting: _"At scale, the variables compound. Calibration window narrower than modelled."_ It is unsigned.

**Folder 2 — Undertow Programme: Field Test Report (Year 3, Final)**

Written in institutional language that requires reading carefully. The shape of what happened is visible:

- A live test was conducted beneath the city. The test site is referred to by grid code only. DC 16 History or local knowledge identifies the grid code as corresponding to what is now the Smokestack Row district — the same area the party descended into during Sessions 17 and 18.
- The energy release was not directed as planned. The record states: _"Structural event, sub-level collapse, personnel lost (number sealed under Protocol Seven), containment invoked. Lattice nodes rendered inert by emergency suppression."_
- The programme was suspended immediately. The suspension order notes: _"All physical infrastructure to be decommissioned. Research materials to be sealed under Deep Research classification. No continuation without full Council authorization and written review."_
- At the bottom of the suspension order: the authorizing signature. A name. It is partially redacted — ink blacked over the personal name, leaving only the title. The readable portion: **"Magister Primus, Deep Research."** That position is now held by Verrath. Eleven years ago it was held by someone else. The name is gone. This is not an accident.

**Folder 3 — Phase II: Continuation Request (Undated)**

A single document, filed at the back of the Suspended Research subsection. A formal reinstatement request citing eleven years of additional theoretical refinement and claiming the original failure was a calibration error, now corrected. It details a revised methodology — more nodes, distributed load, a relay system rather than a single point of release.

The request has no approval stamp.  
The request has no Council authorization.  
It has been filed anyway.

At the bottom, in the Project Status field, a handwritten addition in different ink — recent, not yet faded:

**"Lattice active."**

No date. No signature.

## Samson's Review — Room 7, Floor 2

Ravos is already there when Samson arrives. He is precise, polite, and already has notes open on the desk. He does not offer a greeting beyond Samson's name.

### Phase 1 — The Pretext (~5 minutes)
Ravos reads the court report back to Samson, summarizes the conduct at issue, and asks for Samson's account. He listens without interrupting. He takes one note. He does not react to anything Samson says here.

### Phase 2 — The Real Questions (~10–15 minutes)
Ravos sets the report aside. "A few additional questions, unrelated to the court matter." He then asks, in order:

1. "Have you had contact recently with anyone outside the Arcanum in a professional or academic capacity? Consultants, external scholars, archivists from other institutions?"
2. "Have you noticed anything unusual in the Scholar's Descent or surrounding districts? Arcane disturbances of any kind?"
3. "You were in the Smokestack Row area at the time of the incident. Is that a district you frequent?"

He asks these as neutral administrative questions. He writes down whatever Samson says. He does not react visibly to any answer.

**If Samson was briefed (prep done):** He sees what Ravos is doing. His prepared answers are technically accurate and reveal nothing. DC 11 Deception — low, because his preparation was good. The review ends with Ravos writing "no further action at this time." Samson leaves cleanly and can rejoin the party.

**If Samson was not briefed (prep skipped):** The pivot from the court incident to these questions is abrupt and purposeful. Samson must improvise. Each of the three questions requires a DC 15 Deception to answer without giving Ravos something. Failed checks do not trigger immediate consequences — Ravos does not escalate here — but his final note changes, and Phase 3 reflects it.

### Phase 3 — The Close

**If the review went cleanly:** "Thank you for your time. We will be in touch if anything further is required." Samson is free to go.

**If Samson gave something away:** The same dismissal, but as Samson reaches the door: _"One more thing. If you do encounter anything unusual in the city — arcane disturbances, structural anomalies — the Arcanum maintains a reporting mechanism. I would encourage you to use it."_ Said helpfully. Not helpful. Samson now knows Ravos is watching him specifically.

**Timing complication:** If Samson's review runs long and the Relos distraction is in effect (the faculty meeting), Ravos is supposed to be in that meeting. If Samson's review is still ongoing when the meeting is called, Ravos may attempt to reschedule — or he may send a deputy to the meeting and continue the review. Either way, the distraction window shrinks. If Ravos finishes the review before the distraction ends, he returns to his floor 3 office — which is adjacent to Room 14. If the party is still on floor 3, they are one corner from walking into him.

## Consequence Escalation

### Alert Level 1 — Noticed

A guard sees something odd. He does not act immediately but he asks a question or watches for a moment longer than comfortable. Choose the version that fits the moment:

_"Are you looking for something?"_  
_"This area is for faculty. Do you have an appointment?"_  
_"I'll need to see your authorization."_

**Cover story available (DC 13 Deception or Persuasion):** The guard is satisfied. He moves on. Alert stays at 1 but he will remember their faces if he sees them again.

**No cover story or failed check:** Alert → 2.

### Alert Level 2 — Sought

A guard is now actively searching. He knows someone unauthorized is in the building. He may not know who. At this point:
- A second guard joins any patrol sweep in progress
- The elevator is manually locked at the ground floor; it will not move until the situation is resolved
- Vos is notified; he begins reviewing the visitor log and any construct incident reports

Every time the party moves between areas: DC 15 Stealth, or an encounter with the searching guard.

**If the searching guard finds them:** He tells them they need to come with him to reception. Calmly, firmly.

- **They comply:** See Ejection below.
- **They run or refuse:** Alert → 3.

**Ejection (from Level 2 compliance):**  
Vos identifies them from the log or the construct report. He is not angry. He informs them that they have accessed an area they were not authorized to be in, that this has been noted, and that they are asked to leave the building. If they took anything from the archive and it is found on their persons, it is confiscated. They are escorted to the entrance. Their names are in an incident report. The Arcanum now knows their faces. They will not be welcomed back.

> _Vos, escorting them out:_ "I am sure this was a misunderstanding. The Arcanum takes unauthorized access to restricted areas seriously. I trust this will not be repeated." He holds the door open. He does not raise his voice.

### Alert Level 3 — Caught

Direct confrontation. This triggers if they are found in the archive with documents, if they resist ejection at Level 2, or if Ravos personally encounters them somewhere they should not be.

**They cooperate:** Detained briefly at reception while Vos makes contact with the appropriate authority. Released after 20–30 minutes with a formal written warning. Any materials taken from the archive are confiscated. The Arcanum considers them a known quantity and will be watching. Verrath will likely be informed by the end of the day.

**They run:** Chase. Three DC 14 Athletics or Acrobatics checks in sequence to reach the main entrance or service exit. Two Arcanum Guards (use Veteran stat block, CR 3) pursue immediately. Success on all three: they are out. Failure on any: caught and detained. Running is always an option — the building is large and they know the exits.

**They fight:** Two Arcanum Guards respond within two rounds. If combat continues past three rounds, a Mage (CR 6) arrives. The party can still escape during this window — fighting through is not required, just surviving long enough to reach an exit. Aftermath: the Arcanum is actively hostile. Verrath is informed immediately. This door is permanently closed.

> **Note on combat tone:** The guards are doing their jobs. They are not evil. If the party disengages and runs, the guards pursue to the exit and no further — they do not chase into the street. This is not a kill scenario. It is a failure state with lasting consequences, not a TPK.

## What They Bring Back

If the search succeeded, the party has:
- Confirmation that the Undertow Programme ran a live test beneath what is now Smokestack Row — they were standing above it in Zone 3
- Confirmation that Phase II has no authorization and is active anyway
- The words _"Lattice active"_ in recent ink, with no date
- A partially-redacted name on the suspension order — readable as a title, not a person yet

When they bring this to Elira:

> _She reads the Phase II document. She does not read it again. She sets it on the table and sits with it for a long moment._
>
> _"No approval stamp," she says. Not a question._
>
> _She looks at the window._
>
> _"He filed it because he does not need the approval anymore. The lattice is already running."_
>
> _A pause._
>
> _"Then we are out of time to be careful."_
